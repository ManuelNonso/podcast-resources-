Part One
Discover — What Is Already Inside You
Most people have never been asked the right questions about their own gifts. This section does exactly that. You are not building something new here. You are excavating what has always been present — often hiding in plain sight, taken for granted because it is so native to you.

"An orange seed already contains the full blueprint of an orange tree. The seed does not need to become an orange — it already is one. What it needs is not more potential. It needs the right environment for what is already inside it to finally show."

— Episode 45, The Power of LIFE Podcast
📋 How to use this section

Work through each exercise in order. Do not rush. The value is not in completing the page — it is in the quality of your honesty. If an answer comes quickly and feels easy, push one layer deeper. The most important answers in this section will feel slightly uncomfortable to write.

1
What do people consistently reach for you for — without being asked?
Not your job title. Not what you have trained for. What do the people around you — colleagues, friends, family, clients — naturally turn to you for when something real is at stake? List everything that comes up. Do not filter.
2
What do you take completely for granted that others find genuinely difficult?
The things that feel obvious to you — so natural that you barely notice doing them — are often your deepest gifts. You have lived with them so long they feel ordinary. What do you do effortlessly that you regularly see others struggle with?
💡 Coaching Tip

If you are struggling to answer Exercise 2, ask someone who knows you well: "What do you think I make look easy that most people find hard?" Their answer is usually the most accurate mirror you will find.

3
Where do you completely lose track of time?
Not enjoyment — absorption. The mode of working or creating where you look up and hours have passed. Where you feel most like yourself when you emerge from it — more energised, not less. This is not about what you love. It is about where you disappear into the work.
The Intersection
Where Your Three Answers Meet
Your seed lives at the intersection of your three answers above. The gift that appears in all three — or in the overlap of at least two — is the most accurate signal of your specific, irreplaceable capacity. In the space below, write what you notice when you look across all three answers together.
What pattern or theme runs through all three of your answers?
What keeps showing up? What is the thread connecting what people reach for you for, what you take for granted, and where you lose yourself in the work?
💎 The Diamond Reframe

A rough diamond does not need to become a diamond. It already is one. What it requires is a cutter who understands its specific structure — where the facets lie, where the light will break through — and applies precisely the right pressure in precisely the right place. You are not being transformed here. You are being revealed.

Your Coaching Session

Three layers. Answer each one honestly before moving to the next.

Layer One — Surface Awareness
"If someone who loved you completely — who had no agenda and no fear — looked at your life right now, what gift or capacity in you would they say you are still sitting on? Not performing at the level it deserves. Not fully expressed. Present, but quiet."
Layer Two — Root Identification
"When did you first learn — not in words, but in the way children absorb the rules of their world — that it was not safe to be fully what you are? What was the moment, or the season, when your seed was told — directly or indirectly — to stay in the ground?"
Layer Three — Separation & Reorientation
"What evidence already exists in your own life — not what you hope might be true, but what is verifiably, undeniably true — that directly contradicts the story that you are not enough, not ready, not the real thing?"
If something shifted while answering those questions — sit with it before you move on. That shift is not a distraction. It is the most important data point in this entire workbook.
Part Two
Define — Name Your Seed with Precision
Vague potential stays dormant. The moment you name your gift precisely — specifically enough that it could belong to no one else — it becomes something you can act on. This section builds your personal Seed Profile and your four-move deployment plan.

🌱 Your Seed Profile

Complete each field with the precision it deserves. Vague answers are a signal to go one layer deeper. Every field in this profile should feel undeniably, specifically yours.

The Test: Read your sentence and ask — could this line appear on a generic CV or belong to most professionals in your field? If yes, it is still too general. Rewrite until the answer is clearly no.

Your Framework

The Seed Deployment Framework — Your Version

Move Four

Soil Audit
Where Is Your Seed Getting the Right Conditions?
An orange seed in the wrong soil does not grow — not because the seed is defective, but because the environment cannot support it. Audit your current environments honestly. This is not about burning anything down. It is about seeing clearly.

✅ Right Soil — Receiving My Gift

Environments, relationships, or contexts where your gift is visible, valued, and actively used.

⚠️ Wrong Soil — Keeping the Seed Underground

Environments that are too small, don't value what you carry, or actively suppress your full expression.

⚠️ Important

This audit is not a permission slip to abandon responsibilities or relationships carelessly. It is a clear-eyed assessment. You do not have to act on everything you see here today. But you must see it clearly — because what you cannot name, you cannot change.

📱 The Smartphone Analogy

When you open the box on a brand new phone, everything it will ever need to function is already inside it — the camera, the processing power, the connectivity. Built in by design. But if you never learn the full interface, you spend your life using it at two percent of its capacity. Not because the phone is broken. Because no one ever showed you what it was actually built to do. You are not broken. You have not learned your full interface yet. That changes today.

Part Three
Pressure Test — Does Your Framework Hold When It Gets Hard?
Every framework sounds good in the calm of a coaching session. The question is whether it holds when the old fears show up — when the room does not applaud, when someone does not see what you are carrying, when the easy path is to make yourself small again. These three scenarios test your framework under realistic pressure.
📋 How to use this section

Read each scenario carefully. Do not rush to the "right" answer. These scenarios are designed to surface the real fears — the ones that live beneath the intellectual commitment to growth. Your honest response to each one is more valuable than a polished answer.

Scenario 1 — The Invisible Room

When the environment cannot receive you

You have made the commitment. You have named your gift. You have identified the right soil. And then you step into a meeting, a conversation, a pitch — and the room does not see it. They are polite. They are not hostile. But what you brought was not received. The sideways glances. The polite disinterest. The same room that would have dismissed Susan Boyle before she opened her mouth.
What is your honest first instinct in that moment — the thing you actually do, not the thing you think you should do?
What would it look like to respond from your seed identity — rather than from the old pattern — in that exact moment?
Scenario 2 — The Familiar Voice

When the old story comes back

It is 11pm. You have had a difficult day. Something did not land the way you intended. And the old voice — the one you identified in Part 1, the one that learned its story from an environment that was not built to receive you — shows up. Clear, familiar, and very convincing. "Who do you think you are? You are not actually that good. Everyone else can see it. You have been lying to yourself."
What does this voice specifically say to you? Write it out — the actual words, not a sanitised version.
Using the evidence you identified in Part 1, what is the direct, specific counter-argument from your own life?
What is the one sentence you will say to yourself — out loud if necessary — in that moment to interrupt the pattern?
Scenario 3 — The Safe Path

When comfort offers you an easy way out

Three weeks in. You are tired. The new environment that was supposed to be the right soil is requiring more of you than you expected. The old environment — the one that kept the seed underground but felt familiar and manageable — is offering you an easy return. Nothing dramatic. Just a slightly safer choice. A smaller yes. A quieter version of yourself that fits better in the room. The path of least resistance is right there, and it is genuinely easier.
What is the specific version of this scenario that is most likely to show up in your life over the next 30 days?
What will you do in that moment to hold the commitment — not through willpower alone, but through a practical decision you make now?
🌱 The Seed Does Not Wait for Perfect Conditions

The seed pushes toward the light with what it has. It does not wait for guaranteed success, a validated stage, or the approval of the room. It grows in the direction of what it already knows it is. Susan Boyle sang in church for years — with no stage, no industry, no validation — because the gift did not wait for permission. Neither should you.

Part Four
Protect — Lock In the Commitment and Build the Conditions
Insight without commitment is just a good conversation. This section converts everything you have worked through into a specific, signed, 30-day commitment — with an audit schedule, an accountability protocol, and a final reflection that closes the session.

The Seed Deployment Commitment

A personal contract. Written by you, for you. Sign it when you mean it — not before.
  • I commit to naming my gift clearly and without apology — in the spaces that ask for it and in the spaces that have not yet learned to.
  • I commit to deploying consistently before I feel ready — knowing that readiness is built through deployment, not before it.
  • I commit to auditing my soil — and making at least one deliberate move toward a better environment in the next 30 days.
  • I commit to interrupting the old pattern when it shows up — using the specific counter-argument I identified in Part 3.
  • I commit to holding this work seriously — not as motivation, but as a decision I have already made and intend to keep.

🤝 Your Accountability Protocol

The research on behaviour change is consistent on one point: a commitment witnessed by someone else is significantly more likely to be kept than one made in private. Name your accountability partner now — before you close this workbook.

Final Reflection

Before you close this workbook, one last set of questions. Do not rush these. They are not a summary exercise. They are a closing of the session — the moment you integrate what you discovered today and decide what you are going to do with it.
What is the single most important thing you discovered about yourself in this session today?
What has keeping your seed underground been costing you — specifically, in the areas of your life that matter most?
One year from today, if you deploy your seed consistently and fully — what does your life look like that it does not look like right now?
What is the one action — the smallest possible but most meaningful step — that you will take in the next 24 hours as a direct result of this session?
