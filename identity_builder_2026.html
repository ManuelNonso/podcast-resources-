Identity Statement Guidelines

✅ GOOD Identity Statements:

  • "I am someone who creates valuable content consistently"
  • "I am someone who finishes what they start"
  • "I am someone who invests in their health daily"
  • "I am someone who builds real relationships"
  • "I am someone who takes courageous action despite fear"

❌ AVOID These (They're Goals, Not Identity):

  • "I want to post content 3x per week" (This is a goal)
  • "I will finish more projects" (Still a goal)
  • "I'm going to work out daily" (Still a goal)

Notice the difference? Identity is WHO YOU ARE. Goals are WHAT YOU DO. When you become the right person, the actions follow naturally.

⚡ Part 5: Identity Habit Stacks

You don't build identity through willpower. You build it through systems. Habit stacking makes your identity inevitable.

The Habit Stacking Formula

Example:

After I pour my morning coffee, I will write for 30 minutes.

After I finish my workout, I will capture one content idea.

After I close my laptop for the day, I will journal three wins.
