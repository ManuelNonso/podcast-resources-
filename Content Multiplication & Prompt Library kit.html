Template 1: Authority/Teaching Post
Use this to position yourself as the expert and teach your framework
COPY THIS

[Hook - Problem or question]

Most [your audience] struggle with [specific problem]. Here's why:

[Explain the root cause in 2-3 sentences]

Here's what actually works:

The [Number]-Step [Your Framework Name]:

1️⃣ [Step 1 with brief explanation]
2️⃣ [Step 2 with brief explanation]
3️⃣ [Step 3 with brief explanation]

[Why this works / Expected outcome]

[Call to action - question or next step]

Drop a 💡 if this was helpful!

Example:

Most new coaches struggle with pricing their services. Here's why: You're basing price on time, not transformation.

Here's what actually works:

The Value-Based Pricing Framework:
1️⃣ Identify the specific transformation you create
2️⃣ Calculate what that transformation is worth to them
3️⃣ Price at 10-20% of that value

When you price on value, not hours, everything changes.

What's been your biggest pricing challenge? 👇

⚡ Your 90-Minute Weekly Workflow

Follow this exact workflow every week. Print this page and keep it visible.

Monday: Create Your Pillar (30-60 minutes)

Choose ONE format and create your pillar content:

Option 1: Record a Training (Easiest)
  • Pick one topic from your content calendar
  • Record 10-15 minute teaching on your phone
  • Don't edit for perfection - authentic beats polished
  • Save the video file
Option 2: Write an Article
  • Pick one topic
  • Write 500-700 words
  • Use one of the templates above
  • Focus on one key insight or framework
Option 3: Host a Live Session
  • Go live in your community or on social
  • Teach one concept or do Q&A
  • Record it automatically
  • Save the recording

Tuesday: Batch Repurpose (30 minutes)

Step 1: Transcribe (if video/audio)

Use Otter.ai, Rev.com, or AI tool to transcribe. (5 min)

Step 2: Use AI to Repurpose

Use the AI prompts above to create:

  • Community post (5 min)
  • Newsletter version (5 min)
  • Social media posts (3 platforms) (10 min)
  • Quotes for graphics (5 min)
Step 3: Add Your Voice

Review AI output. Add personal touches, stories, and your unique perspective. (5 min)

Wednesday: Schedule Everything (30 minutes)

Use scheduling tools:
  • Later, Buffer, or Metricool for social media
  • Your email platform for newsletter
  • Post directly to community (or schedule if possible)
Schedule this week's content:
  • Monday: Pillar post in community
  • Tuesday: Instagram/Facebook post
